Dear Society Members, Organizing Committee members,leads and co-chairs,

As another remarkable term comes to a close, the Mathematics Society of the University of Moratuwa looks back with pride and gratitude upon a year filled with academic excellence, leadership, and impactful initiatives. The success of our flagship projects and our academic programs is a direct testament to your tireless dedication and collaborative spirit.

We cordially invite all members of the Mathematics Society, past committee co-chairs, committee leads and committee members who powered our projects throughout the year to join us at the Annual General Meeting (AGM) 2026.

This meeting stands as an essential milestone of our society: we will formally recognize our outstanding projects, distribute Official Service Letters and Certificates of Appreciation to all project contributors and committee members, and proceed with the election and handover of the new Executive Board for the 2026/2027 term.

Certificates & Service Letters

Presentation of certificates to project chairs (Enigma, SLYMC) and committee members, alongside official service letters for all contributors.

Executive Board Handover

Election of the incoming President and Executive Board for 2026/2027, followed by recognition of the outgoing board.
